An AI-Powered Parametric Insurance Platform for Marine Shipment Delays

01About the Project

MARSA is a web-based platform that supports parametric insurance for marine shipment delays. AI agents predict vessel arrival times and delay risk, track the vessel using AIS data, and verify its arrival using satellite imagery, weather and port records. A predefined rule engine then determines eligibility and calculates compensation, with Jeddah Islamic Port as the initial study area.
